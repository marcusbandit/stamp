Stamp is a map of everywhere you have been. Tap a country and mark it visited, lived in, or want to go. The Passport tab counts your countries against the 193 UN members and 2 observer states and splits them by continent.

Let your photos fill it in
Most of your photos already know where they were taken. Stamp reads the location saved in each one, works out the country and the first and last day you were there, and lists what it found. You tick the ones to add. The scan runs on your phone and nothing is uploaded.

Keep as much or as little as you like
A country on its own is a complete entry. When you want more, add visits with a year, a month or exact days, the cities you saw, a heart and a star rating, and the regions you covered. Countries you lived in hold stays with their dates.

Remember the trip
Each visit has a timeline of dated stops. Pin a place by hand or pick a town from over 150,000 bundled cities, reorder the stops, and attach photos, videos and short notes. Stamp suggests photos taken on those dates near that place.

Private by design
No account, no ads, no tracking, and no internet permission. Your marks, notes and photo links stay on this phone. The map is drawn from shapes bundled with the app, so it works on a plane.

Made for every screen
Portrait, landscape and unfolded layouts, so it reads like a book on a foldable and like a map on a tablet.
